## 1. Audience snapshot

We are not pitching Aaron in this room. We are pitching the people who
own Mercor's growth P&L — the team that has to make any partnership
move money. They will not grade us on research-lab fit or RL-pipeline
elegance. They will grade us on five questions:

1. **Creator-side acquisition cost.** How cheap is creator intake?
2. **Brand-side acquisition cost.** This is the marketplace's hardest
   side, and the side where Mercor's GTM is more effective than ours.
3. **Take-rate model.** Does our 20% placement + 5% perf kicker
   structure stand up next to SaaS comps and headhunter benchmarks?
4. **Path to first $1M.** What does the unit-econ stack look like?
5. **Path to first 100 brands.** Where do we win them, and how
   honest are we about what we cannot do alone?

Aaron's deck answers (1) and (3) well. This room needs (2), (4), and
(5) loudest. The talk-track flex below moves day-1 revenue (slide 6)
and a new growth-only slide #9 forward; the brand-acquisition honesty
paragraph is the centerpiece of the script.
